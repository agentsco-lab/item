# Probe C, stage 2: Wayland clients on the panels (2026-09-30)

`probes/wl-panels`: a minimal smithay compositor - smallvil's protocol
handlers (compositor, xdg-shell, shm, seat with a keyboard, output,
data-device), less pointer grabs - whose output is hwcomposer's display 0
through `crates/hybris-hwc` and `crates/smithay-hybris` (the EGL traits of
stage 1, now a crate). One output of 2784x1800 at scale 2: the left panel is
logical x 0-675, the hinge 675-717, the right panel 717-1392. New toplevels
take the panels in turn, each maximized to its panel, as item tiles a window
to the panel it was launched from. smithay with `wayland_frontend`,
`desktop`, `renderer_gl`, `use_system_lib`; libwayland-server opened at run
time (`wayland-sys` with `dlopen`), so the cross build needs no more of the
phone than libxkbcommon.

## It works

- **GL clients' buffers import.** `renderer.bind_wl_display()` succeeded on
  libhybris' EGL, and GNOME Calculator drawing through GL came in as an EGL
  buffer (`surface 5: buffer Some(Egl)`, `2026-09-30-probe-c2c.out`):
  libhybris' `android_wlegl` into smithay's EGL import. The same app with
  `GSK_RENDERER=cairo` came in over shm (`Some(Shm)`).
- **Both windows on their panels**, the shm one on the left and the GL one on
  the right, both shown right.
- **60 fps with two windows**: 59-60 every second, 16.7 ms between frames on
  average, the longest 17-19 ms once they are open (up to 25-45 ms in the
  second a window opens), no hwc errors (`2026-09-30-probe-c2d.out`).

## On the way

- **Upside down and mirrored.** GL draws an EGL surface's default framebuffer
  bottom-up; smallvil gives its winit output `Transform::Flipped180` for
  that. Here the damage tracker renders with `Flipped180` while clients see
  an untransformed output, so touch will not need turning.
- **The clients' environment.** foot is not on the phone's system; GNOME
  Calculator as root without a session bus waited 25 s for portals and never
  opened. Each client now gets its own bus (`dbus-run-session`, also needed
  because the app is single-instance per bus) and `GDK_DEBUG=no-portals`.
  `GDK_DEBUG=opengl` makes GTK ask for `eglQueryDisplayAttribEXT`, which
  libhybris lacks, and libepoxy aborts the app.
- **Checking the frame, not only the numbers**: see stage 1's first run.
