# Step 5b: text on the shade

The shade's seven-segment clock gave way to text: the time, the date in
Russian, the battery (`compositor/src/text.rs`, `shade.rs`).

## How

- Fonts come from the phone's system: Lato Light for the time (64 logical
  px), Noto Sans for the date and the battery (18 and 15). Both have
  Cyrillic.
- [fontdue](https://crates.io/crates/fontdue) rasterizes a line on the CPU at
  the output's scale into premultiplied RGBA. Glyphs are one physical pixel
  each; the size is rounded up to even, so the logical size is whole.
- A `Label` keeps its text in a smithay `MemoryRenderBuffer` (ABGR8888, scale
  2) and remakes it only when the text changes. The renderer uploads it once
  per change. While the sheet moves, only the texture's place in the frame
  changes.
- The shell's elements are `ShellElement` (rectangles and textures) ahead of
  the space's in one frame. Text is `Transform::Normal`, the right way up on
  the flipped output, like the windows.
- The text refreshes when a sheet is taken, and once a second while a sheet
  is out: a new frame only if it changed (the minute, the battery).

## Results

The text is the right way up and sharp, and
the sheet pulls as smoothly as before.

|---|---|
| finger's move to the screen | 23.5-26.5 ms mean, 30 ms at most (29 in one second) |

## Open: two seconds of missed frames

- 13:45:51: 16 missed, present up to 15 ms, finger to screen 42 ms. This was
  the first pull of the run. Likely the first use of the text: the texture
  uploads and the first frames drawing them.
- 13:46:10: 12 missed, present up to 15.6 ms, in the middle of the run. The
  cause is not known.

"present" is hwcomposer's time for our frame, so the stall is on the Android
side or in waiting for the GPU. Frames cost 3 ms of drawing, as before.

To find out: a benchmark that pulls the shade with a virtual finger (like
`tap.py`), and a log of each missed frame with its present and its
elements. The first pull can be warmed up: import the labels' textures at
start.
