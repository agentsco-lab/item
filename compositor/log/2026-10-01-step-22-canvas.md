# Step 22: a buffer of our own, and the tap measured right

## A buffer of our own

Every frame was drawn whole into hwcomposer's window buffer: a partial
redraw into it left garbage in Adreno's tiles (step 6), since the window's
three buffers keep nothing reliable between frames through libhybris.

Now the frame is drawn into a buffer of our own (a GL renderbuffer the size
of the output, `output.rs`, `Canvas`), with a damage tracker of its own at
age 1: our buffer keeps every pixel, so only what changed is drawn. The
whole of it is then copied into the window's buffer (`glBlitFramebuffer`,
0.2-0.5 ms) and swapped. The window's buffers are written whole every
frame, so their contents never matter; no garbage (screenshots of a launch
and of the shade, both panels).

`CANVAS=0` keeps the old way, for comparison. `LOG_TIMES=1` logs each
frame's stages, wall and CPU time: building the elements, binding, drawing,
the copy, the swap; and when a client's commit reaches the loop.

It costs 20 MB of GPU memory for the buffer.

**A tap on the probe** (`tools/ab-latency.sh`, 37 taps each), touch to the
present that holds the new content:

| | median | p90 |
|---|---|---|
| frames drawn whole (`CANVAS=0`) | 7.7 ms | 8.8 ms |
| our buffer | 6.2 ms | 7.9 ms |

The shell's motion (`tools/ab-motion.sh`) is unchanged: gap medians
16.5-17.3 ms, 0-5 gaps over 25 ms a gesture, as in step 21.

Where the 6 ms go, one tap (`LOG_TIMES`): the client's commit reaches the
loop 0.5 ms after it is made; the frame starts 0.3 ms later; building the
elements 1.2 ms (the probe's shm upload in it, `glTexSubImage2D`); drawing
1.7 ms; the copy 0.4 ms; then `eglSwapBuffers` to hwcomposer's present
1.2 ms (validate and present over binder to the composer). Drawing is CPU
time in smithay and Adreno's GL driver (`perf`), not waiting; the swap is
mostly waiting.

## The tap against phosh, measured right

Step 21 took the first present after the probe's commit as the frame with
it, and found phoc 3 ms quicker (4.3 against 7.6 ms). Listing every present
around each touch shows otherwise:

- under phoc each of the 37 taps has **two** presents within 50 ms. The
  first comes 4.3 ms after the touch, often 0.0-0.3 ms after the probe's
  commit: too soon to hold it (an upload and a draw), so phoc draws a frame
  on the touch itself, before the client's frame is in. The second, the
  frame with the content, comes 9.9-25.2 ms after the touch, median 17.6.
- under item-compositor each tap has one present, the one with the content
  (36 of 37; one had a second).

`ab-latency.sh` now lists every present around each touch and takes the
only one, else the second, as the content's.

| touch to the present with the new content | median | p90 |
|---|---|---|
| item on phosh (phoc) | 17.6 ms | 24.4 ms |
| item-compositor | 6.2 ms | 7.9 ms |

The client also gets the touch sooner here: 0.4-0.5 ms from the touch to
the probe's commit, against 3.1 ms under phoc.
