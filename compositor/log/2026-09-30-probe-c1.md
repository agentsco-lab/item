# Probe C, stage 1: smithay's renderer into hwcomposer (2026-09-30)

`probes/smithay-frame` on `crates/hybris-hwc` (probe B's hwcomposer code as a
library): smithay 0.7 with `renderer_gl` only, cross-built on the PC
(`tools/fetch-sysroot.sh` brings the phone's libxkbcommon to link against),
run like probe B from `/tmp` with the shell stopped.

smithay makes its EGL display over GBM, and the Duo's own path has none. Two
small implementations of smithay's native EGL traits were enough:

- `HybrisDisplay: EGLNativeDisplay` - the Android platform
  (`EGL_PLATFORM_ANDROID_KHR`, which libhybris lists) on the default display;
- `HwcWindow: EGLNativeSurface` - `eglCreateWindowSurface` over the
  ANativeWindow that `hybris_hwc::Output` presents to hwcomposer.

The context needs a config (`EGLContext::new_with_config`, GLES 3, 8-bit):
`EGLContext::new` makes a surfaceless one, and the first run stopped there.

## The first run drew the wrong frame

The numbers were right and the picture was not: on screen the bar was
flickering at the left edge instead of running, and a grey background. Read
back from the frame before the swap (`glReadPixels`): the background was the
colour asked for (`3c4b59`), the EGL config's native visual was 1
(RGBA_8888, as the window) and the surface 2784x1800 - but the hinge was not
black and the bar was nowhere. smithay's `draw_solid` takes its damage
relative to its destination, and the probe passed it in output coordinates,
so both rectangles were clipped away except when the bar was near x 0. Fixed
(`Rectangle::from_size(dst.size)`); the frame then held the black hinge
(`1390:000000`) and the bar where drawn (x 2736-2776 for 2735), and on screen
it ran. Probe B and the fixed C1 back to back:
`2026-09-30-probe-c1-fixed.out`.

## It works, at 60 fps

- smithay: "Successfully selected EGL platform: PLATFORM_ANDROID_KHR", EGL
  1.5; the context RGBA8 with depth 24 and stencil 8; `GlesRenderer` up.
- "Dmabuf import extension not available" on this platform: expected, the
  clients' buffers come over `android_wlegl` (stage 2).
- 20 s (`2026-09-30-probe-c1.out`): 59-60 fps, 16.66-16.97 ms between
  presents on average, 6 frames over 20 ms in 20 s (3 in the first second),
  no hwc errors. The same frame as probe B, drawn by `GlesRenderer`: clear,
  two solid rectangles.
- smithay's render takes 1.0-1.5 ms a frame; the swap waits for vsync
  (15.2-15.7 ms).
