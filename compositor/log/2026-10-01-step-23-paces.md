# Step 23: each client told when it suits it

A client animating every frame (the probe's `anim`) saw its frame on
screen two vsyncs after its commit (32.3 ms). Its timeline, from the
probe's own log and the uprobe on hwcomposer's present:

    -0.2 ms  frame callback (sent as our frame F is drawn)
     0.0 ms  commit
    +6.8 ms  present of F, drawn without it
   +23.7 ms  present of F+1, with it; on screen at the vsync after

Frame callbacks go out as our frame is drawn (step 7b), so that a slow
client (Settings, 12-14 ms a frame) has a whole frame to draw its next and
scrolls at 60 fps. A quick one answers in under a millisecond, while F is
still being drawn without it, and waits a frame. Told at the vsync instead
(`CALLBACKS=vsync`), a quick client's commit comes early in the frame, is
drawn at once and shows at the next vsync; but Settings then scrolled at
39-48 fps (step 7b).

## Each client by its pace

`pace.rs` follows each surface's time from its frame callback to its
commit. The quick ones (under 6 ms on average; slow again over 9) hear at
the vsync, once the last frame is out; the rest as the frame is drawn
(`Callbacks::Adaptive`, the default; `CALLBACKS=draw` the old way).

Timing an answer has a catch: while the loop draws, a commit waits unread,
so a commit read as the loop comes free came at some unknown time during
the drawing. Only answers read while the loop was free are timed. Three
unknown ones in a row (a client that answers while we draw, so perhaps a
quick one) earn a trial at the vsync, where the answer is timed for sure;
a trial that shows it slow sends it back, and the next waits 5 s.
`PACE_DEBUG=1` logs every callback and answer.

| | before | after |
|---|---|---|
| an animating client, commit to screen | 32.3 ms (two frames) | 15.5-16.0 ms (one frame) |
| Settings scrolling, frames a second (misses) | 57-60 (0-3) | 58-61 (0-3) |
| a tap, touch to the present with its content | 6.2-6.8 ms | 6.7 ms |
| the shell's motion, gap median | 16.5-17.3 ms | 16.5-16.9 ms |

The probe turns quick 60 ms after its window maps. Settings is tried once,
answers in 19 ms and stays as it was; between scrolls, idle, it may answer
quickly and be tried again, without a frame lost. GNOME Calculator answers
in 0.2 ms and is quick.

## What else was looked at

- **The drawing itself** (1.7 ms of CPU a tap): smithay's frame ends with
  an EGL fence and a flush, then our copy and the swap flush again: two
  submissions to the GPU a frame. One would take rewriting smithay's
  `render_output`, for about a millisecond. Not now.
- **GL instancing off** (`INSTANCING=0`): no difference (6.4 against 6.8
  ms, within the noise).
- **The CPUs' clocks at their maximum**: a tap 6.1 ms against 6.2. Not the
  lever.
