# Probe C, stage 3: touch (2026-09-30)

`probes/wl-panels` with smithay's `backend_libinput`: a libinput context on
libinput's path interface (the probe runs as root and opens the device
itself), `LibinputInputBackend` in the event loop, and touch passed to the
window under the finger over `wl_touch`; a touch also raises that window and
gives it the keyboard. The cross build links libinput from the phone
(`tools/fetch-sysroot.sh`).

## The touchscreen maps one to one

The Duo's touchscreen covers both panels and the hinge, 2784x1800 physical,
and so does the output. So touch needs no gap in a layout, as sway and phoc
did on the Lindroid chain: libinput's position transformed to 1392x900 is
the logical point on the output.

## The device: sfduo-pen-split's

The first two runs saw no touch at all. libinput had opened
`surface_touchscreen`, but the port's `sfduo-pen-split` grabs that raw
digitizer (EVIOCGRAB: `evtest` said "This device is grabbed by another
process") and gives the fingers and the pen devices of their own, `sfduo
touchscreen` and `sfduo pen`. The probe now picks the device by name, `sfduo
touchscreen` first and the raw one if the port's is not there.

## It works

Both calculators tapped by hand: they work on both panels. Touches landed
where the fingers were - x 941-947 on the right panel (it starts at 717),
x 246-341 on the left - each on a window; libinput gave TouchDown, TouchUp
and TouchFrame. 60 fps throughout, no hwc errors
(`2026-09-30-probe-c3.out`).
