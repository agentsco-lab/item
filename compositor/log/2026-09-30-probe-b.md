# Probe B: a GL frame through hwcomposer from Rust (2026-09-30)

`probes/hwc-frame`: Rust, no crates, libhybris opened with dlopen,
cross-built on the PC (`cargo build --release --target
aarch64-unknown-linux-gnu`, glibc 2.34 needed, the phone has 2.43). Run on
the phone's installed system from `/tmp` with the shell stopped
(`tools/probe-run.sh 20 /tmp/hwc-frame 20`: phosh and the composer watchdog
stopped, the Android composer restarted clean, phosh back after). Nothing
installed on the phone.

The recipe is libhybris' `test_hwcomposer`: hwc2 display 0 with one client
layer over it, `HWCNativeWindowCreate` (the C API of libhybris'
hwcomposer window, so no C++ shim) under an EGL surface on
`eglGetDisplay(EGL_DEFAULT_DISPLAY)` with `EGL_PLATFORM=hwcomposer`, and in
the window's present callback: the buffer's acquire fence into
`set_client_target`, `present`, the release fence onto the buffer and the
present fence onto the previous one.

## It works, at 60 fps across both panels

- hwcomposer: display 0, 2784x1800, vsync period 16.667 ms (60 Hz).
- GL: OpenGL ES 3.2 V@0502.0, Adreno (TM) 640.
- The frame: the whole 2784x1800 redrawn every frame - a background drifting
  in colour, the hinge's columns black, a white bar sweeping across both
  panels every 2 s. On screen the bar runs sharp.
- 20 s (`2026-09-30-probe-b.out`): 59-60 frames every second, the time
  between presents 16.66-16.71 ms on average, the longest 17.3-18.8 ms but
  twice 21.7 ms; 2 frames over 20 ms in 20 s; no hwc errors; one frame per
  vsync.
- `eglSwapBuffers` takes 16.4 ms: with three buffers and swap interval 1 it
  waits for the next vsync. A compositor has to draw late in the frame and
  keep input off that thread.

Against the Lindroid chain (duo-lindroid, `log/2026-09-30-probe-kwin.md`):
cage with both panels animating ran at 30 fps, 33 ms between frames and
50-125 ms at worst, half its frames over 25 ms, because of create-disp's CPU
copy. Here there is no copy, the GPU's fence goes to hwcomposer, and the
frame is on time.
